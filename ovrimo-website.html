About Us

We're Ovrimo.
We Build Things.

Ovrimo builds thoughtful digital products designed to simplify everyday work and life. We create modern apps and tools focused on clarity, usefulness, and clean design.

Our Story

Started with a simple idea

Ovrimo LLC was founded on a straightforward belief: the best software gets out of your way. We create modern apps and tools focused on clarity, usefulness, and clean design — in a world full of bloated apps and feature-chasing products, we wanted to build something different.

We started with productivity. Specifically, the problem of staying focused and organized without needing a complex system to manage the system. ModeDo was our first answer to that question — available now at modedo.app.

Our products are built to feel simple, reliable, and genuinely helpful. Ovrimo is independently run — no VC pressure, no pivot cycles. Just thoughtful software we're proud of, growing sustainably from there.
